# Historical experiment scripts

These scripts record early environment pilots, observation ablations, local GPU
scheduling, interrupted-job recovery, and older VLA batch orchestration. They were
imported from the original research checkout and are retained for traceability.

They preserve their original contents, including machine-specific paths, process
management commands, and references to their former `scripts/` locations. They
are archival records, not portable launchers; relocation does not make them safe
to execute on a new machine.

For the paper's current entry points, use the [script guide](../README.md) and
[reproduction guide](../../docs/reproduction.md). Earlier research notes are in
[`docs/history/`](../../docs/history/). Original paths and file hashes are recorded
in the [source manifest](../../docs/migration/aws-source-files.json).
