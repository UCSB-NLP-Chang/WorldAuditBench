# Harry Potter Hard Cover Book

Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/harry-potter-hard-cover-book-8adc1ed0908849aeabfb2af63e0bc461
Author: Kaali (CaliKal)
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
License link: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

The original GLB was downloaded by the user. Adaptations: trimmed scanned floor remnants; simplified geometry from 157,922 to 17,802 triangles; oriented the book flat; imported its original cover texture into Unreal; capped runtime texture size at 4096; configured a rough paper/printed-cover material and collision. The adapted book asset remains under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Original cover artwork and text are retained in the scan; this attribution does not assert ownership of the Harry Potter book or artwork.
